Which case material is best for everyday use?

Glass cases have a tempered glass back panel set in a soft TPU frame — the sharpest print quality and a premium finish. They work well for everyday use because the soft sides and corners absorb most impact, but glass adds a little weight and can crack in a hard fall.

Soft silicone cases are the grippiest and most forgiving option if you drop your phone often. Hard cases are the thinnest and lightest, best suited to bold artwork rather than heavy-duty protection.

Premium Glass Cover Designs for a Modern Look

A glass cover is a tempered glass back panel set into a soft TPU frame. Glass holds print far better than plastic — colours stay saturated, the surface resists scratching, and the finish stays glossy instead of clouding the way a printed hard case can after a few months in a pocket.

What people tend to like about them:

  • Vibrant, high-detail print quality
  • Smooth glass finish that resists scratching
  • A more premium look and hand-feel than plastic
  • Soft TPU sides that take the knocks
  • Raised camera and screen lips

Worth knowing: glass adds a little weight, and like any glass panel it can crack in a hard fall. If you drop your phone often, a soft silicone case is the more practical choice.

Printed and transparent glass cases

The range covers printed glass cases with full-bleed artwork, and transparent glass back covers that leave your phone’s original finish on show while keeping it scratch-free. Both use the same tempered glass panel and TPU frame.

Model coverage runs across iPhone and Samsung as well as most current Android devices — popular picks include glass cases for iPhone 13, iPhone 15 and the Samsung S series. Find yours on the glass phone cases by model page.

Not Sure Which Case to Pick? Here Is How to Choose

Every phone user is different, and so is every case. The quickest way to decide is to match the case to how you actually handle your phone:

  • Glass cases — for the best-looking print and a premium finish. Choose this if you want the phone to stand out and you are reasonably careful with it.
  • Soft silicone cases — for grip, flexibility and shock absorption. The most practical pick for everyday use, and the one to choose if you drop your phone regularly.
  • Hard printed covers — for bold artwork on the slimmest possible profile. Better for style than for serious protection.
  • Pop-holder cases — for one-handed use and hands-free viewing, with the grip built in.

Still deciding? Think about how often you actually drop your phone, whether you prefer a slim or a grippy feel, and which look you would be happy seeing every day for the next year.
